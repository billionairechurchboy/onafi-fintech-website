About Onafi

Making Financial Services Easier to Access and Use.

Financial processes should help people and businesses move forward, not create unnecessary complications.

Our story

Technology that keeps money moving.

We are building digital financial tools that make everyday transactions and business payments easier to manage.

Our goal is to give people and businesses a more convenient way to handle their financial activities through technology.

The name Onafi comes from ọ̀nà, the Yoruba word for “way” or “path”, combined with fi for finance. It reflects what we want to be for our customers: a clearer way forward with money.

Our mission

To make useful financial technology more accessible, practical and easier to use.

What guides us

The principles behind every product.

Simplicity first

If a financial task can be made simpler, we make it simpler. Fewer steps, clearer screens, less guesswork.

Trust is earned

We handle financial information with care and communicate clearly — especially when things don’t go to plan.

Built for real businesses

From market traders to multi-country organisations, we design for how businesses actually operate.

Access for everyone

Useful financial tools shouldn’t be reserved for the few. We build for individuals and businesses of every size.
