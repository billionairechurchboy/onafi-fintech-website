FAQ

Questions about plans.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can start on Starter and move to Growth whenever your business needs more. For Enterprise, our sales team will work with you on the move.

Is there a setup fee?

Fees and terms are shared clearly during sign-up, before you commit to anything. There are no surprises.

Do I need a website to accept payments?

No. With payment links you can collect payments through WhatsApp, social media, email or SMS — no website required.

Can personal account holders use Onafi?

Absolutely. Individuals can open a personal account to receive, send and track money. Learn about Personal.

What does Enterprise onboarding include?

A structured onboarding plan, technical integration support and a dedicated contact who understands your organisation’s requirements.
