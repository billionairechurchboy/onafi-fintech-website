  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ONAFI_TEST_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "email": "customer@example.com",

  }'

Help Centre

Common questions.

How do I open an account?

Select Get Started, choose a personal or business account and follow the steps. You’ll be asked to verify some details to keep your account safe.

How long do transfers take?

Most transfers are processed quickly. You’ll see the status of each transfer in your dashboard and receive a notification when it completes.

How do I issue a refund to a customer?

Open the transaction in your dashboard and choose “Refund”. You can refund the full amount or part of it.

Can I add team members to my business account?

Yes, on Growth and Enterprise plans you can invite team members and assign roles and permissions.

I need more help. Who can I contact?

Our support team is ready to help. Contact support and we’ll get back to you.

Can’t find what you’re looking for?

Get in touch and our team will point you in the right direction.
